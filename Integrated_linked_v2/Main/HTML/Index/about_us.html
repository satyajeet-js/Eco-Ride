Our Story

About EcoRide

We are on a mission to make Pune greener, healthier, and better
connected — one ride at a time.

A greener way to move

CycleGo started in 2024 with a simple idea: what if every person in Pune
could grab a bicycle within two minutes of stepping out of their home? No
traffic, no parking hassle, no pollution — just the joy of riding.

Today we operate 250+ cycles across 35+ stations in Pune, serving over
10,000 riders every month. Our cycles have replaced thousands of short cab
rides and helped cut tonnes of CO₂ from the city's air.
