Frequently asked question

What is Eco Ride?

Pay As You Ride has a ₹10 unlock fee, then ₹5 for every 15 minutes of riding. Daily and Monthly passes include unlimited rides within their time limits.

How can I find a station?

How can I check my ride history?

How can I make a payment?

Daily and Monthly passes waive the ₹10 unlock fee entirely. Pay As You Ride includes it in every ride.
