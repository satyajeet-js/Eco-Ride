Getting Started

How CycleGo Works

From finding a cycle to completing your ride — four simple steps, all from your phone.

⌖
01

Find a Station

Open the CycleGo app and find the nearest station on the interactive map. See real-time bike and slot availability before you head out.

▣
02

Scan QR Code

Every CycleGo bicycle has a QR code on the frame. Scan it with the app to unlock instantly — no keys, no dock, no waiting.

♧
03

Start Riding

The lock releases and you are ready to go. Track your ride duration, distance, and fare in real time as you explore the city.

□
04

Return Anywhere

Park the bicycle at any CycleGo station, lock it, and end the ride in the app. Payment is automatic and your receipt is ready instantly.
